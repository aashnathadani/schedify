Your best-fit schedules

Set your requirements on the left, block off times you don't want classes below, then hit Generate. Schedify searches for conflict-free combinations and shows you its top 5, ordered for compactness.

Getting the most out of Schedify

  • Not loving these five? Hit Generate again -- it re-samples, so a new batch of valid combinations usually turns up.
  • Spot an elective you like? Add it to your Required courses so it's locked into every future batch, then Generate again to build the rest of the schedule around it. Repeat until it's exactly right.
  • Seeing classes at times you don't actually want? Nothing here was blocked off yet -- go add those times to the blocked-time grid below, then hit Generate again to rule them out.
